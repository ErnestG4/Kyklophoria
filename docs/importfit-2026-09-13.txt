What it says
------------
The band limit is not what is costing import. At K=64 the median waveform is
inside the first 64 harmonics to four figures, and only 5.4% of the corpus
loses more than a tenth of itself to the band limit at all.

The sine-only projection is. A node is signed coefficients on sin(h.theta), so
whatever sits in the cosine half is discarded — and after the rotation search
has done its best, a *third* of the corpus still loses more than a tenth of the
wave, and a fourteenth of it loses more than a quarter. The median goes 94.8%
against a ceiling of 100.0%; the tenth percentile goes 77.7% against 96.0%.

That gap is what a phase-correct representation would buy, and it is the whole
of it: nothing else in the chain is anywhere near as lossy.

Caveats
-------
The fit is a fraction of *energy*, which is not a fraction of audibility. A
wave keeping 77% of its energy does not sound 77% right — the discarded part
is the phase relationship between partials, which is exactly what makes a saw
a saw rather than a stack of sines with a saw's spectrum. The earlier
measurement of that is in README.md: a saw's magnitudes at random phase
correlate 0.79 with a saw.

AKWF is not a neutral sample of "waveforms people import". It is a curated
corpus, heavy on single cycles ripped from hardware, and it is what our own
README points people at. It is the right corpus for this question and it is
still one corpus.
