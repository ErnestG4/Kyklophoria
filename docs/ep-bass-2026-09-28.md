# Why ep-vel's bass reads an octave up — and the refit that would fix it

28 September, overnight, no GPU used. For Combust to decide on; nothing
refitted.

## What was reported

ep-vel's bass (G2 to C#4) plays an octave up at hard velocity (open since the
22nd, "fit_shaped/pickup").

## What it is

`tools/notecheck.py` could not find a layered world's recordings, so it graded
every -vel world on the note asked for (fixed: 190946b, 32a32c9). Graded on
the recordings, over E2 to D#4:

- the **recordings** read their own notes;
- **ours** read the octave on 7 notes struck softly (0.3) and 14 struck hard
  (0.9);
- the balance says why: our 2nd harmonic is within a few dB of the
  recording's, but the 3rd and 4th are 20-40 dB short. A pitch detector —
  and an ear — finds the fundamental's period from the 3rd and 4th; without
  them it hears the 2nd.

The fitter's own figures say the same (`out/fit/ep-vel/fits.tsv`, bark
columns, h2/h3/h4 over h1 soft then hard). G3 (epv019):

|  | soft | hard |
|---|---|---|
| recording | +13 / -1 / -5 | +23 / +8 / +16 |
| fit | +12 / -30 / -27 | +18 / -19 / -10 |

## Why the fit is there

The fits put the tine almost dead centre on the pickup: h/w 0.014 to 0.03
across the bass (column h_over_w). A symmetric field around a centred tine
doubles the frequency — the 2nd harmonic — and a small swing adds little
else. Swept on the runtime's own pickup at the fitted voicing (G3), no swing
reproduces the recording: small swings match the 2nd and leave the 3rd 30 dB
short; swings big enough for the 3rd put the 2nd and 4th 20-30 dB over.

Off-centre, the same pickup reaches the recordings. A grid over voicing and
swing on the runtime's pickup (scratch tool, G3's width, coil and gain):

| target (h2/h3/h4) | best h/w | swing (widths) | reached | off, all three |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G3 soft +13/-1/-5 | 0.20 | 0.82 | +8/-1/-5 | 5.5 dB |
| G3 hard +23/+8/+16 | 0.10 | 1.43 | +22/+7/+18 | 3.2 dB |
| A2 soft +9/+0/-8 | 0.35 | 1.08 | +4/-1/-8 | 5.9 dB |
| A2 hard +19/+10/+13 | 0.30 | 2.88 | +13/+5/+13 | 11 dB |

So the model can hold the instrument; the optimiser found the centred local
optimum (the 2nd harmonic is easy to match there) and stayed.

## The refit, if wanted

`fitvel.py --only` the bass records (epv004-epv026, E2-D#4) seeded at h/w
about 0.2 and a swing about one width, instead of the centred start — or with
a prior on h/w away from zero for this set. A handful of records, minutes on
the 4090. Checked afterwards with `notecheck.py ep-vel ... --vel 0.3` and
`--vel 0.9` against the recordings, and by ear against today's.
