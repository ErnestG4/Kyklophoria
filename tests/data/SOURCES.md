# Where the test fixtures came from

Every `.kykm` in this directory is a *fitted* world, and a fitted world is
not only numbers: a point carries a **burst**, which is a window of the
recording the point was fitted from — tens of milliseconds of the attack,
band-split and faded. Publishing a `.kykm` publishes that audio. So every
fixture here has to come from a source whose terms allow it, and this file
is the record of which.

The rule: nothing in `tests/data/` may derive from a recording we cannot
redistribute. `tests/run.sh` refuses to run if a fixture has no entry below.
That is a reminder, not a licence audit — it cannot tell whether an entry is
*true*. If you add a fixture, put the source in it and mean it.

## The fixtures

| file | points | fitted from | terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| `piano.kykm` | 11, MIDI 22–103 | University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples, piano, ff | free for any use |
| `bodies.kykm` | 4, a body row | University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples, mallets | free for any use |
| `tine.kykm` | 11, a velocity set | generated by ModalBake's own `epi` — no recording at all | ours, AGPL-3.0 |

**University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples**, Electronic Music Studios,
recorded under Lawrence Fritts. `https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html`,
checked 2026-09-22, which says of them:

> these recordings have been freely available on this website and may be
> downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions.

That is a grant of permission in plain words rather than a named licence,
so it is quoted here in full rather than reduced to an SPDX identifier.

**`tine.kykm` is synthetic.** ModalBake generates the velocity set itself and
fits its own output, so its bursts are our audio and carry no third party's
terms. It is the one fixture here that would survive any licence question,
and the reason the velocity checks use it.

## What was here before, and is not

Until 2026-09-22 this directory held `wurli.kykm`, `ep-vel.kykm` and
`perc.kykm`, fitted from commercial and non-commercial-only libraries — a
CNCD Wurlitzer, a commercial EP library, and Philharmonia percussion, which
is CC BY-NC-SA. Three of the goldens in `tests/golden/` were rendered from
them. None of that was redistributable and all of it was pushed public. The
fixtures above replace them note for note in what they test; the goldens were
re-rendered. See the commit that removed them for what had to be done to the
history.

## Goldens

`tests/golden/m4_*.wav` are renders of the fixtures above and inherit their
terms. The rest of `tests/golden/` is synthesis from `core/` with no
recording anywhere in it.
